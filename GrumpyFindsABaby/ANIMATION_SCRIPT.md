# Grumpy Finds a Baby — Animation Script

## Production setup

- **Frame:** 16:9 landscape, 1920×1080 delivery
- **Frame rate:** 24 fps
- **Estimated runtime:** 55–65 seconds
- **Visual direction:** follow `STYLE_GUIDE.md` and the watercolor-and-ink treatment from `../Milo-Makes-Room/STYLE_GUIDE.md`
- **Character lock:** Milo remains newborn-sized in every shot and is always fully supported by Grumpy
- **Animation approach:** gentle 2D storybook motion, small facial changes, slow camera moves, subtle watercolor texture, and restrained parallax
- **Audio:** one warm narrator, quiet room tone, soft piano or music-box accents, and a distinct camera click in Scene 5
- **Transitions:** soft watercolor dissolves of 10–14 frames

## Scene 1 — A Tiny Sound

**Duration:** 9 seconds

**Narration:** “Grumpy heard cooing, so gentle and low. ‘What makes such a sound? I just have to know!’ He followed the squeak, and what did he see? A baby as small as a baby could be!”

**Picture:** Grumpy sits in his office chair and discovers tiny Milo cradled in his arms.

**Motion:** Milo curls his fingers and blinks. Grumpy's surprise melts into a smile.

**Camera:** Begin on the quiet room, then ease toward the pair.

**Sound:** Soft infant coo, faint room tone, one curious piano phrase.

**Transition:** Warm cream watercolor wash.

## Scene 2 — Careful Hands

**Duration:** 9 seconds

**Narration:** “He tucked one big hand beneath the small head, and lifted the bundle in cream, gray, and red. The baby looked up. Grumpy started to grin. ‘Well, look what I found—let our story begin!’”

**Picture:** In the sunny kitchen, Grumpy lifts Milo just high enough for their eyes to meet.

**Motion:** Grumpy adjusts his supporting hands. Milo's tiny feet curl inward and his eyes find Grumpy's face.

**Camera:** Slow upward drift from Grumpy's hands to their shared gaze.

**Sound:** Sweater rustle, quiet baby breath, gentle music lift.

**Transition:** Dissolve through window light.

## Scene 3 — Ten Tiny Fingers

**Duration:** 9 seconds

**Narration:** “He counted ten fingers and toes, every one. Then Milo stared back as if Grumpy were fun. He reached for the whiskers that grew on his chin. Grumpy held still with a ticklish grin.”

**Picture:** Grumpy counts one tiny hand while Milo reaches toward his beard.

**Motion:** One finger uncurls at a time. Grumpy's eyebrows rise when Milo touches a whisker.

**Camera:** Gentle push toward Milo's hand and Grumpy's smile.

**Sound:** Soft counting rhythm in the music and a tiny amused hum.

**Transition:** Short ink-line ripple.

## Scene 4 — A Closer Look

**Duration:** 8 seconds

**Narration:** “Milo looked left. Then he looked right. He studied his Grumpy with all of his might. A nose, two eyebrows, a soft beard below—‘That’s everything, baby. Now you know!’”

**Picture:** Milo examines Grumpy's face while Grumpy laughs.

**Motion:** Milo's gaze travels across Grumpy's face; his hand pats the beard. Grumpy's shoulders bounce once with a quiet laugh.

**Camera:** Small side-to-side move following Milo's inspection.

**Sound:** Two playful woodwind notes and a warm chuckle.

**Transition:** Soft paper-texture dissolve.

## Scene 5 — The First Pictures

**Duration:** 9 seconds

**Narration:** “The camera went click! What a picture to see: a baby with Grumpy, as snug as could be. They took one, then two, then a few more that day, while Milo kept finding new reasons to stay.”

**Picture:** Grumpy and Milo turn toward the unseen camera in the home office.

**Motion:** Grumpy settles Milo safely against his sweater. Milo blinks toward the lens.

**Camera:** Hold nearly still like a family portrait; add two brief exposure glows.

**Sound:** Two gentle camera clicks followed by a bright music-box phrase.

**Transition:** The second exposure glow fades into the final scene.

## Scene 6 — Found Each Other

**Duration:** 10 seconds

**Narration:** “Grumpy found a baby—or so the tale goes—with ten tiny fingers and ten tiny toes. But safe in those arms, Milo knew from the start: he had found his Grumpy—and Grumpy’s whole heart.”

**Picture:** Sleepy Milo rests safely against Grumpy's cheek and chest in the warm kitchen.

**Motion:** Milo's breathing gently lifts the cream blanket. Grumpy closes his eyes for one quiet beat and holds him closer.

**Camera:** Very slow pull back, ending on the complete embrace.

**Sound:** Quiet heartbeat under the final piano cadence.

**Transition:** Hold for two seconds, then fade to warm paper white.
