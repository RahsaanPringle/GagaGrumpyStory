# Grumpy Finds a Baby — Visual Style Guide

## Current artwork

- **Reference guide:** inherit the series style from `../Milo-Makes-Room/STYLE_GUIDE.md`
- **Style family:** comic watercolor picture book
- **Medium:** flat digital color with soft watercolor washes and subtle paper texture
- **Linework:** bold, smooth black outlines on characters; lighter painted edges in the room
- **Color:** burgundy, coral-orange, cream, honey wood, turquoise, and warm daylight
- **Lighting:** bright natural window light with very soft shadows
- **Characters:** rounded cartoon forms, large expressive eyes, and close affectionate poses
- **Milo's approximate age:** newborn or very young infant; tiny enough to fit securely in Grumpy's forearms and unable to sit, stand, crawl, or support his own head
- **Backgrounds:** simplified home office and vintage kitchen based on the photographs in `references/originals/`
- **Composition:** separate landscape illustrations with one clear emotional beat per image
- **Mood:** surprised, tender, playful, safe, and deeply affectionate

## Character continuity

Grumpy has short dark-and-gray hair, a short salt-and-pepper beard, a burgundy cable-knit sweater, and gray pants. Infant Milo has a large round baby head, sparse pale copper hair, plump cheeks, tiny hands and feet, and short curled limbs. He wears a coral-orange long-sleeve onesie, pale patterned pants, and a loose cream knit layer or blanket.

Every hold must look safe. Grumpy supports Milo's head, neck, back, and bottom. Keep Milo dramatically smaller than Grumpy and consistent in size across every scene.

## Direction for new artwork

Follow `../Milo-Makes-Room/STYLE_GUIDE.md`. Use the selected photographs in `references/selected/` for poses, clothing, and room details. Use `../Characters/Grumpy.png` for Grumpy's identity, while retaining the burgundy sweater shown in the photographs.

## Avoid

Toddler or preschool proportions, thick hair, teeth, independent sitting or standing, unsupported holds, adult-sized limbs, altered clothing, photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, hard cel shading, cluttered backgrounds, embedded titles, and watermarks.
