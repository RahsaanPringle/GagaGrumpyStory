# Grumpy Finds a Baby — YouTube Description

Grumpy hears a tiny coo and follows the sound—only to discover baby Milo waiting to be held. As Grumpy counts ten tiny fingers and ten tiny toes, Milo studies his face, reaches for his whiskers, and finds a safe place in Grumpy’s arms.

**Grumpy Finds a Baby** is a warm illustrated story about the beautiful moment a baby and his Grumpy find each other—and the beginning of a love that fills Grumpy’s whole heart.

Made with love for Milo and the Grumpy who was waiting to be found.
