# Baby Milo's First Snow — Animation Script

## Production setup

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps
- **Estimated runtime:** 75–90 seconds
- **Visual direction:** Follow `STYLE_GUIDE.md`; use the canonical characters in `../Characters/` and keep Milo about 1 year old.
- **Animation approach:** Gentle 2D storybook motion, soft watercolor texture, clean ink outlines, light snowfall, and restrained parallax. Keep Milo's bundled clothing and the dogs' markings consistent between shots.
- **Audio:** Warm adult narrator, gentle winter ambience, soft piano and woodwind accents, and clear read-aloud sound effects.
- **Transitions:** Soft 10-frame dissolves, with sound bridges for the snow sounds.

## Scene 1 — A white world

- **Duration:** 11 seconds
- **Narration:** “Milo woke and looked outside. The yard was quiet, soft, and white. He pressed both hands against the glass. The yard was all white—where had the green grass gone?”
- **Picture:** Gaga holds Milo at the window as he studies the snow-covered yard.
- **Motion:** Milo pats the cool glass with both hands; slow flakes drift past the window. Gaga points out the familiar fence beneath its blanket of snow.
- **Camera:** Begin close on Milo's hands, then ease out to reveal the white yard.
- **Sound:** Quiet winter wind, a soft piano note, and a tiny curious hum.
- **Transition:** Dissolve through the pale window light.

## Scene 2 — Bundled for outside

- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Narration:** “Gaga zipped Milo's coat and tucked his scarf in tight. She warmed his little mittens and made sure they fit just right. ‘We'll look together. I'll stay close to you.’”
- **Picture:** Gaga bundles Milo in a warm coat, hat, scarf, and mittens beside the open door.
- **Motion:** Gaga checks his hat and mittens, then carries him carefully onto the porch.
- **Camera:** Gentle tilt from the bundled-up Milo to the snowy doorway.
- **Sound:** Coat zip, mitten rustle, and the muffled hush of falling snow.
- **Transition:** Match dissolve from the porch step to pawprints.

## Scene 3 — Where did the grass go?

- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Narration:** “Ruby bounded out with a happy hop. Riley stepped carefully—stop, shake, stop! Ruby sniffed the snow. Riley shook each paw. ‘The grass is gone!’ they seemed to say.”
- **Picture:** Riley and Ruby explore the yard while Gaga holds Milo securely.
- **Motion:** Ruby makes a playful bound and leaves a small trail of prints. Riley tests the snow with one paw, lifts it, and shakes off the flakes.
- **Camera:** Low tracking shot across the dogs' tracks, ending on Milo's delighted face.
- **Sound:** Crunch, patter, sniff, shake-shake, and a soft dog collar jingle.
- **Transition:** Pawprints lead into Grumpy's snowman-building.

## Scene 4 — A snowy visitor

- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Narration:** “Grumpy rolled one snowball, then two, then three. He stacked them up carefully. ‘A visitor!’ cried Ruby. Riley stood very still. The snowman just smiled while the dogs sniffed and stared.”
- **Picture:** Grumpy makes a small, friendly snowman as the dogs investigate from a safe distance. Gaga holds Milo nearby.
- **Motion:** Grumpy pats the snowman's round body into place and adds a carrot nose. Ruby bounces once; Riley watches and sniffs the air.
- **Camera:** Pan from Grumpy's gloved hands up to the snowman's face and the dogs' reactions.
- **Sound:** Snow crunches, a gentle plop, curious sniffs, and one playful musical accent.
- **Transition:** Snowflakes drift across the frame into the next close-up.

## Scene 5 — One tiny snowflake

- **Duration:** 11 seconds
- **Narration:** “A snowflake floated down and landed on Milo's mitten. He held it up and looked so near. It sparkled for a moment—then disappeared. ‘Again!’ said Milo, with a smile from ear to ear.”
- **Picture:** Close on one snowflake resting briefly on Milo's mitten as Gaga supports him.
- **Motion:** Milo turns his mitten to see the flake melt; he reaches toward a few drifting flakes while staying warm in Gaga's arms.
- **Camera:** Slow push toward the mitten, then tilt up to Milo's amazed smile.
- **Sound:** A tiny bright chime, soft breath, and quiet falling snow.
- **Transition:** The chime carries into the warmth of the house.

## Scene 6 — Warm again

- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Narration:** “Inside, Gaga rubbed Milo's hands and wrapped him in a blanket. Ruby curled by the fire; Riley settled down. Grumpy brought warm cocoa for the grown-ups. Outside, the little snowman kept watch.”
- **Picture:** The family relaxes together in the warm room while the snowman is visible through the window.
- **Motion:** Gaga gently warms Milo's mittened hands; Ruby curls up and Riley lies down. Grumpy sets mugs on the table, and flakes continue to fall outside.
- **Camera:** Move from Milo's cozy blanket to the family, then settle on the snowman beyond the window.
- **Sound:** Quiet fire crackle, soft blanket rustle, and a warm piano melody.
- **Transition:** Slow dissolve to the final window view.

## Scene 7 — First snow

- **Duration:** 8 seconds
- **Narration:** “The yard was white. The room was warm. Milo had found a new wonder in every flake. His very first snow—and a day to remember.”
- **Picture:** Milo watches the snowman from Gaga's lap as the family gathers close.
- **Motion:** Milo gives a small wave toward the window; the dogs rest nearby as snow drifts past the snowman's smiling face.
- **Camera:** Slowly pull back to include Milo, Gaga, the family, and the snowy window.
- **Sound:** Gentle music resolves beneath the quiet winter ambience.
- **Transition:** Hold, then fade to warm cream.
