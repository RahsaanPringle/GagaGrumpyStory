# Baby Milo's First Snow — Visual Style Guide

## Current artwork

- **Style family:** comic watercolor picture book
- **Medium:** flat digital color with soft watercolor washes and paper-like texture
- **Linework:** bold, smooth black outlines around characters; lighter edges in backgrounds
- **Color:** bright warm pastels, especially sky blue, cream, coral, orange, and olive green
- **Lighting:** high-key daylight and warm indoor ambient light; very soft shadows
- **Characters:** rounded cartoon proportions, large expressive eyes, simple readable expressions
- **Milo's approximate age:** about 1 year old; a curious baby beginning to explore the world
- **Backgrounds:** simplified, airy domestic interiors with broad watercolor shapes
- **Composition:** one clear emotional beat per image; characters grouped tightly and readable at thumbnail size
- **Mood:** safe, affectionate, optimistic, and gently humorous

## Direction for new artwork

Use this story as the main series style: polished watercolor-and-ink illustration with clean outlines, soft pigment texture, warm natural light, and friendly cartoon proportions. Match characters to `../Characters/` and geography to `../Scenes/Terrain-Storybook.png`. Riley is the larger dog with the green collar; Ruby is the smaller dog with the red collar.

Draw Milo at about age 1: baby proportions, a round youthful face, and curious expressions as he experiences snow for the first time. Keep his striped shirt, Gaga's cream cardigan and blue shirt, Grumpy's orange sweater, and Mama Murdle's coral dress consistent with their character files. Dress Milo warmly over his usual clothes for outdoor scenes. Use 16:9 landscape scenes unless a page layout calls for another crop.

## Avoid

Photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, anime styling, hard cel shading, flat vector-only artwork, heavy black backgrounds, excessive detail, altered character ages, and changing established clothing or collar colors.
