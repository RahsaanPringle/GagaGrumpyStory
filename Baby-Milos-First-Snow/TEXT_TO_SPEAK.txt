Milo woke and looked outside. The yard was quiet, soft, and white. He pressed both hands against the glass. The yard was all white—where had the green grass gone?

Gaga zipped Milo's coat and tucked his scarf in tight. She warmed his little mittens and made sure they fit just right. “We'll look together. I'll stay close to you.”

Ruby bounded out with a happy hop. Riley stepped carefully—stop, shake, stop! Ruby sniffed the snow. Riley shook each paw. “The grass is gone!” they seemed to say.

Grumpy rolled one snowball, then two, then three. He stacked them up carefully. “A visitor!” cried Ruby. Riley stood very still. The snowman just smiled while the dogs sniffed and stared.

A snowflake floated down and landed on Milo's mitten. He held it up and looked so near. It sparkled for a moment—then disappeared. “Again!” said Milo, with a smile from ear to ear.

Inside, Gaga rubbed Milo's hands and wrapped him in a blanket. Ruby curled by the fire; Riley settled down. Grumpy brought warm cocoa for the grown-ups. Outside, the little snowman kept watch.

The yard was white. The room was warm. Milo had found a new wonder in every flake. His very first snow—and a day to remember.
