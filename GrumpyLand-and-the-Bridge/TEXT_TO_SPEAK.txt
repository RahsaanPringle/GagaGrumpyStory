The Bridge Across GrumpyLand. Sometimes a gate is more than wood and chain.

GrumpyLand was vast and wide. Grumpy's bridge crossed close at hand; the road bridge stood beyond his land. Two bridges crossed the river blue—but each had a job to do.

Bob the Biker saw the bridge beyond the gate. He asked Milo to open up so he could ride across to the other side. Milo thought that sounded okay, so he unlatched the gate.

Ruby and Riley saw a bicycle roll through. Their tails went thump and their paws went tap. They thought a new friend had come to play. Bob realized this was not the shortcut he expected.

Bob grabbed his bicycle and hurried toward the gate. Ruby bounced and Riley flew—they thought Bob was playing too. His shortcut ended right away.

Grumpy called the dogs to stay. Milo closed the gate. Grumpy explained that the dogs only wanted to have fun, but big dogs could make a bike ride bumpy. Bob nodded and realized he should have asked who guarded the land.

Grumpy explained that people build bridges to help us cross, fences to guard against loss, and gates for reasons that may not be clear. Before opening a gate or tearing down a fence, learn what it protects. Understanding first is respect.
