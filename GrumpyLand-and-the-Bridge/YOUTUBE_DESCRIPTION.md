# The Bridge Across GrumpyLand — YouTube Description

Grumpy and Milo explore the bridges, stream, fences, and gate on GrumpyLand. When Bob the Biker asks to ride through the gate, Milo opens it—and Ruby and Riley think their new visitor has come to play! Grumpy helps everyone get safely back where they belong and explains why bridges, fences, and gates each have a purpose.

**The Bridge Across GrumpyLand** is an adventurous, gently funny family story about boundaries, safety, and understanding what a rule protects. Before opening a gate or crossing private land, ask permission and check with a trusted adult.

Made with love for Milo and the Grumpy who was waiting to be found.
