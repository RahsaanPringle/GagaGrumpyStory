# The Bridge Across GrumpyLand — Visual Style Guide

## Current artwork

- **Style family:** glossy high-detail digital children's illustration
- **Medium:** smooth digital painting with saturated color, modeled volume, and crisp environmental detail
- **Linework:** clean dark contours on characters and props; backgrounds rely more on painted edges
- **Color:** bright sky blue, vivid green, honey wood, teal, yellow, and warm stone
- **Lighting:** strong sunny daylight with polished highlights and clear cast shadows
- **Characters:** highly animated cartoon figures with large eyes and energetic poses
- **Milo's approximate age:** about 6 years old; an early school-age child trusted with simple responsibilities but still learning when to ask an adult
- **Backgrounds:** expansive rural landscapes with detailed water, bridges, flowers, gates, stonework, and distant hills
- **Composition:** separate 16:9 landscape images; wide establishing shots mixed with action-focused scenes
- **Mood:** adventurous, energetic, humorous, and instructive
- **Continuity note:** the corrected image set uses the canonical character designs throughout: Grumpy in orange and olive, six-year-old Milo in his striped shirt, Ruby with a red collar, Riley with a green collar, and Bob in teal cycling clothes with a yellow helmet.

## Direction for new artwork

Use the shared watercolor-and-ink series style, the canonical characters in `../Characters/`, and the property layout in `../Scenes/Terrain-Storybook.png`. Draw Milo at about age 6 with early school-age proportions: longer limbs and a little more height than in the preschool stories, while keeping his recognizable face and hair. Grumpy wears his orange sweater and olive pants; Milo has copper-red hair and his striped shirt; Riley and Ruby are German Shepherds with green and red collars. Bob matches `../Characters/Bob-the-Biker.png`.

Keep the private footbridge, public road bridge, stream, wooden gate, fences, and rural paths visually distinct. Use separate 16:9 landscape images rather than a sprite sheet.

## Avoid

Alternate dog breeds, alternate character ethnicities or ages, generic farm characters, photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, changing bridge locations between scenes, modern urban scenery, and crowded compositions that obscure the gate or bridge lesson.
