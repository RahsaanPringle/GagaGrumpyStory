# The Bridge Across GrumpyLand — Animation Script

## Production setup

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps
- **Estimated runtime:** 85–105 seconds
- **Visual direction:** Follow `STYLE_GUIDE.md`; use canonical Grumpy, Milo, Riley, Ruby, and Bob. Milo is about 6 years old. Use `../Scenes/Terrain-Storybook.png` for geography.
- **Animation approach:** Watercolor-and-ink adventure animation with clear spatial continuity among gate, private footbridge, stream, and public road bridge. Keep chase motion energetic but safe and non-threatening.
- **Audio:** Warm narrator, rural ambience, light adventure score, bicycle sounds, paws, water, and gate hardware.
- **Transitions:** Map-like pans, stream ripples, and action wipes.

## Scene 1 — Title and GrumpyLand

- **Duration:** 8 seconds
- **Narration:** “The Bridge Across GrumpyLand. Sometimes a gate is more than wood and chain.”
- **Picture:** Grumpy, Milo, Riley, and Ruby overlook the property, stream, gate, and two bridges.
- **Motion:** Stream flows, trees sway, dogs scan the land, and Grumpy points out the route.
- **Camera:** Slow aerial push toward the family and gate.
- **Sound:** Water, birds, opening adventure theme.
- **Transition:** Camera follows the stream downstream.

## Scene 2 — Far and wide

- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Narration:** “GrumpyLand was vast and wide. Grumpy's bridge crossed close at hand; the road bridge stood beyond his land. Two bridges crossed the river blue—but each had a job to do.”
- **Picture:** Grumpy shows Milo the private footbridge and distant public road bridge.
- **Motion:** Grumpy points first near, then far; Milo follows with his gaze; water and leaves move gently.
- **Camera:** Wide pan connecting both bridges without changing their positions.
- **Sound:** Flowing stream and light rural ambience.
- **Transition:** Pan follows the lane back to the closed gate.

## Scene 3 — Bob at the gate

- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Narration:** “Bob the Biker saw the bridge beyond the gate. ‘Please open up. I’ll ride across to the other side.’ Milo thought that sounded okay, so he unlatched the gate.”
- **Picture:** Bob stands with his blue bicycle outside the gate; Milo stands safely inside.
- **Motion:** Bob gestures politely; Milo considers, reaches up, and opens the latch.
- **Camera:** Shot-reverse-shot across the gate, ending on the latch.
- **Sound:** Bicycle freewheel, birds, metal latch click.
- **Transition:** Gate swings open across the frame.

## Scene 4 — Time to play

- **Duration:** 13 seconds
- **Narration:** “Ruby and Riley saw a bicycle roll through. Their tails went thump and their paws went tap. They thought a new friend had come to play. Bob only thought, ‘Oh, dear!’”
- **Picture:** The dogs run happily toward Bob while Milo remains near the open gate.
- **Motion:** Riley takes long strides; Ruby bounces; Bob brakes, eyes widening, and begins to turn around.
- **Camera:** Dog-height tracking shot toward Bob, then cut to his reaction.
- **Sound:** Tail thumps, paw beats, brake squeak, comic musical stop.
- **Transition:** Bob lifts the bicycle into the next scene.

## Scene 5 — The fastest shortcut

- **Duration:** 13 seconds
- **Narration:** “Bob grabbed his bicycle and hurried toward the gate. Ruby bounced and Riley flew—they thought Bob was playing too. His shortcut ended right away.”
- **Picture:** Bob carries the bicycle toward the exit as the dogs follow playfully.
- **Motion:** Bob jogs carefully; dogs bound behind at a safe distance; Milo steps clear of the opening.
- **Camera:** Side tracking shot ending outside the gate.
- **Sound:** Footsteps, paws, bicycle rattle, lively percussion.
- **Transition:** Gate closes with a firm but calm click.

## Scene 6 — Safe again

- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Narration:** “Grumpy called the dogs to stay. Milo closed the gate. ‘They only wanted fun,’ Grumpy explained, ‘but big dogs make a bike ride bumpy.’ Bob nodded. ‘I should have asked who guards this land.’”
- **Picture:** Bob catches his breath outside; family and dogs remain safely inside.
- **Motion:** Dogs sit on Grumpy's cue; Milo secures the latch; Bob steadies the bicycle and waves.
- **Camera:** Balanced composition with the gate dividing the safe spaces.
- **Sound:** Breathing settles, latch clicks, music calms.
- **Transition:** Grumpy kneels beside Milo as Bob rides away in the distance.

## Scene 7 — Bridges, fences, and gates

- **Duration:** 21 seconds
- **Narration:** “Grumpy said, ‘People build bridges to help us cross, fences to guard against loss, and gates for reasons that may not be clear. Before opening a gate or tearing down a fence, learn what it protects. Understanding first is respect.’”
- **Picture:** Grumpy teaches Milo beside the closed gate while Riley and Ruby listen; Bob uses the public road bridge in the distance.
- **Motion:** Grumpy points to bridge, fence, and gate in turn; Milo nods; dogs settle; Bob rides safely across the distant bridge.
- **Camera:** Medium family shot that slowly widens to show the full connected landscape.
- **Sound:** Stream, breeze, gentle closing theme, faint bicycle bell.
- **Transition:** Fade to the illustrated terrain view.
