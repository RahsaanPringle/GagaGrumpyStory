# Ruby & Riley's Dog-Tired Day — Visual Style Guide

## Current artwork

- **Style family:** softly painted digital storybook
- **Medium:** digital gouache with subtle canvas texture and gently modeled forms
- **Linework:** thin dark-brown outlines, with some edges defined by value instead of ink
- **Color:** warm ochre, teal, muted green, burgundy, and honey-colored interior light
- **Lighting:** soft natural daylight outdoors; warm, cozy lamplight indoors
- **Characters:** semi-realistic anatomy with enlarged eyes and softened child-friendly faces
- **Milo's approximate age:** about 1 year old; a baby or very young toddler who is carried and bundled in a blanket
- **Backgrounds:** believable home and yard spaces with moderate detail and clear depth
- **Composition:** six individual cinematic illustrations; medium and wide shots
- **Mood:** playful, homey, restful, and affectionate

## Direction for new artwork

Move toward the shared watercolor-and-ink series look used in `../Scenes/00-cover.png`. Preserve the cozy lighting and story clarity, but use the canonical designs in `../Characters/`. Draw Milo at about age 1 with baby proportions: a large head, short limbs, soft round features, and assisted or carried movement. Riley remains larger with a green collar; Ruby remains smaller with a red collar. Use the shared terrain reference for outdoor geography.

Create each story scene as its own landscape image with consistent framing and no embedded story text.

## Avoid

Photorealistic fur or skin, inconsistent dog size, camouflage collars, overly dark interiors, dramatic action blur, glossy 3D surfaces, and changing established clothing or character ages.
