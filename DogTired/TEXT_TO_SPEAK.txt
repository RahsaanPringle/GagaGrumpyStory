Ruby and Riley were two very good dogs, and their favorite little boy was Milo.

One morning, Gaga bundled Milo in his blanket and promised they would be back that afternoon. The house became much too quiet.

Perhaps Grumpy would play. But Grumpy—who was only resting his eyes—was already asleep. Ruby nudged his slipper. Riley licked his hand. Grumpy only snored louder. Hooonk… shooo!

So the dogs found their favorite rope. Ruby pulled east. Riley pulled west. Then—pop! The rope slipped free, and both dogs tumbled into a heap.

They chased a red ball, yellow leaves, a speedy squirrel, and even their own tails. They dug, rolled, splashed, and raced until Ruby’s paws felt heavy and Riley’s ears began to droop.

At last, a key jingled. Gaga came home with Milo. Ruby opened one sleepy eye, and Riley’s tail gave one tiny thump.

Soon Ruby was tired. Riley was tired. Milo was tired. The whole house became quiet and warm.

They were all completely, absolutely… dog tired.
