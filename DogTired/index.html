Ruby and Riley were two very good dogs with big pointy ears and two thump-thump-thumping tails.

Their favorite little boy was Arlo.

One morning, Gaga bundled Arlo into his blanket. “We’ll be back this afternoon,” she promised.

Ruby and Riley watched them leave. The house suddenly felt much too quiet.

Wake Up, Grumpy!

“Perhaps Grumpy will play with us,” said Ruby.

But Grumpy was already asleep in his favorite chair.

HOOONK… SHOOO! HOOONK… SHOOO!

Ruby nudged his slipper. Riley licked his hand. Grumpy only snored louder.

Then came a tiny sound from beneath the blanket:

Pfffft!

Ruby and Riley looked at each other. “Outside,” they agreed.

The Great Rope Battle

Waiting made the morning move as slowly as a turtle wearing heavy boots.

“Let’s play until Arlo comes home!” barked Riley.

They grabbed their favorite rope. Ruby pulled east. Riley pulled west.

They tugged and growled and shook their heads until—

POP!

The rope slipped loose, and both dogs tumbled backward into a heap of paws and tails.

Run, Race, Tumble!

Next, they raced into the yard.

They chased a red ball, three yellow leaves, one speedy squirrel, and—just for a moment—their own tails.

Ruby zoomed around the oak tree. Riley bounced through a pile of leaves.

They ran until their ears flapped, their tongues hung out like pink neckties, and even the squirrel looked tired from watching them.

Every Game They Knew

They dug one excellent hole. They rolled twice in the grass. They splashed through a muddy puddle.

Shake-shake-shake!

Then they raced back through the house, around the table, beneath Grumpy’s feet, and past his chair.

Grumpy never woke up.

By late afternoon, Ruby’s paws felt heavy. Riley’s ears began to droop.

Arlo Comes Home

At last, a key jingled in the front door.

Gaga stepped inside with Arlo in her arms. “Look who’s home!” she called.

Arlo waved both hands when he saw his dogs.

Ruby opened one sleepy eye. Riley’s tail gave one tiny thump.

They wanted to jump up and welcome their boy—but their legs had turned into noodles.

The Best Kind of Tired

Gaga settled onto the sofa with Arlo. He yawned a little yawn, snuggled into her arms, and closed his eyes.

Ruby rested her head beside Riley. Grumpy kept right on snoring.

Soon Gaga’s eyes grew heavy too.

Ruby was tired. Riley was tired. Arlo was tired. The whole house was quiet and warm.

They were all completely, absolutely… dog tired.

And together was the very best place to be.
