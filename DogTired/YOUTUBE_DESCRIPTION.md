# Ruby & Riley’s Dog-Tired Day — YouTube Description

When Gaga leaves for the day with Milo, Ruby and Riley suddenly find the house much too quiet. Grumpy is fast asleep, so the two dogs make their own fun—with a rope battle, a red ball, flying leaves, muddy paws, and every game they know.

By the time Milo comes home, Ruby and Riley are too tired to give him their usual bouncy welcome. Soon the whole family is together again, cozy, happy, and completely, absolutely… dog tired.

**Ruby & Riley’s Dog-Tired Day** is a warm illustrated family story about missing someone, making your own fun, and discovering that together is the very best place to rest.

Made with love for Milo, Ruby, Riley, Gaga, and Grumpy.
