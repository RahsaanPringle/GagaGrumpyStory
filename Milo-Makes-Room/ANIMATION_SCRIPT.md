# Milo Makes Room — Animation Script

## Production setup

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps
- **Estimated runtime:** 75–90 seconds
- **Visual direction:** Follow `STYLE_GUIDE.md`; use the canonical characters in `../Characters/` and keep Milo about 5 years old.
- **Animation approach:** Gentle 2D storybook motion, layered parallax, restrained character acting, soft watercolor texture, and clean ink outlines. Preserve faces and clothing between shots; avoid morphing.
- **Audio:** Warm adult narrator, light acoustic score, quiet room tone. Dialogue may be voiced separately or read by the narrator.
- **Transitions:** Soft 10-frame dissolves, except where a page-turn transition is specified.

## Scene 1 — Title and family

- **Duration:** 7 seconds
- **Narration:** “Milo Makes Room. A Gaga and Grumpy story.”
- **Picture:** The family gathers around the blue heart blanket. Riley sits at left; Ruby sits at right.
- **Motion:** Blanket corners lift gently, tails wag, eyes blink, and the family exchanges smiles.
- **Camera:** Slow push from a room-wide view to Milo and the blanket.
- **Sound:** Soft music begins; faint tail thumps.
- **Transition:** Page turn into Mama Murdle's announcement.

## Scene 2 — Big news

- **Duration:** 11 seconds
- **Narration:** “Mama Murdle had wonderful news. ‘A baby is growing in my belly. You’re going to be a big brother!’”
- **Picture:** Mama Murdle stands with one hand on her belly as the family gathers around her.
- **Motion:** She gestures warmly; Milo's eyes widen; Grumpy raises both fists in delight; the dogs perk up.
- **Camera:** Medium group shot with a gentle push toward Milo's reaction.
- **Sound:** Warm musical lift and one happy dog bark.
- **Transition:** Match dissolve from Mama's hand on her belly to hands moving furniture.

## Scene 3 — Making room

- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Narration:** “Gaga moved a basket. Grumpy moved a chair. Everyone was making room for someone who wasn’t there.”
- **Picture:** The family rearranges the sunny room for the baby.
- **Motion:** Gaga carries a basket; Grumpy slides a chair; Milo watches; Ruby trots through the activity while Riley stays calm.
- **Camera:** Slow side pan following the movement across the room.
- **Sound:** Basket rustle, chair scrape, playful pizzicato notes.
- **Transition:** Music softens as the camera settles on Milo alone.

## Scene 4 — Milo's worry

- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Narration:** “If the baby needed room, where would Milo go? Would there still be room for him? Milo didn’t know.”
- **Picture:** Milo sits quietly near the edge of the room while the activity blurs softly behind him.
- **Motion:** His shoulders lower, his feet turn inward, and his gaze moves from the empty baby space to his family.
- **Camera:** Slow push to an eye-level close-up; background parallax becomes still.
- **Sound:** Score reduces to a few soft piano notes.
- **Transition:** A measuring tape flicks into frame from the next shot.

## Scene 5 — Grumpy measures

- **Duration:** 11 seconds
- **Narration:** “‘We have plenty of room!’ said Grumpy. ‘I measured it myself!’ He measured the sofa, the chair—and nearly measured Ruby’s tail!”
- **Picture:** Grumpy cheerfully measures everything while Ruby's wagging tail crosses the tape.
- **Motion:** Tape extends and snaps lightly; Ruby turns in a curious circle; Milo begins to smile.
- **Camera:** Medium tracking shot ending on Ruby's tail and Grumpy's surprised face.
- **Sound:** Tape clicks, tail thumps, and a small comic musical accent.
- **Transition:** Quick dissolve into Gaga kneeling beside Milo.

## Scene 6 — Heart space

- **Duration:** 14 seconds
- **Narration:** “Gaga hugged Milo close. ‘When someone new joins a family, love is not cut in two. A heart can stretch and make more room—enough for them, and enough for you.’”
- **Picture:** Gaga hugs Milo as Grumpy, Mama Murdle, Riley, and Ruby gather near.
- **Motion:** Gaga opens her arms and hugs him; Milo relaxes; the family leans closer; dogs settle at their feet.
- **Camera:** Start medium-wide, then gently orbit a few degrees into a close family composition.
- **Sound:** Warm strings enter under the narration.
- **Transition:** Blue heart shapes on the blanket carry into the final shot.

## Scene 7 — Room for Milo

- **Duration:** 13 seconds
- **Narration:** “Milo placed his favorite book beside the blanket blue. His love had not grown smaller. It grew—and grew—and grew. There would always be room for Milo, too.”
- **Picture:** Milo places his book beside the folded blanket while everyone smiles.
- **Motion:** He sets the book down carefully, pats the blanket, then turns toward his family as the dogs move beside him.
- **Camera:** Close on the book and blanket, then pull back to the complete family.
- **Sound:** Music resolves warmly; soft page-close sound.
- **Transition:** Fade to cream with “The End” added by the final editor, not generated inside the artwork.
