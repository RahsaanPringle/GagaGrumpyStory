The Boat That Never Boated

Grumpy had a boat.

It sat in the yard beneath the old shade tree, day after day, with Ruby and Riley keeping watch.

The boat never splashed. It never zoomed. It never even wiggled.

Zzzzzzz…

“Grumpy,” asked Arlo, “why is the boat always snoozing? Why doesn’t it ever go boating?”

Grumpy Explains

Grumpy sat beside the boat and rubbed his beard.

“Well, BabyMan, a good boat boats,” he said. “It starts when you ask. It carries you across the water. It brings you home again.”

Ruby’s ears stood tall. Riley tilted her head.

“But this boat,” Grumpy continued, “is a great big floating bucket of bolts.”

Huff, Puff, Complain

Grumpy remembered the first trip to the lake.

He pulled. The boat coughed.

He pulled again. The boat sneezed.

Huff! Puff! Cough-cough-cough!

Ruby and Riley watched from the shore while the motor grumbled as if Grumpy had asked it to clean its room.

At last, the boat started—very slowly and very unhappily.

Quiet in the Middle

The yard boat chugged away from shore.

Putt-putt-putt…

Then, right in the middle of the lake, it gave one final cough.

Pfffft.

And stopped.

Grumpy pulled the starter. He checked the motor. He asked nicely. He even gave it a very serious look.

The boat refused to boat, so Grumpy paddled the whole floating bucket of bolts back to shore.

A Lemon of a Boat

“I tried again another day,” Grumpy told them. “And another.”

Each time, the boat huffed. It puffed. It complained. Then it waited until the worst possible moment to take another nap.

Grumpy held up a bright yellow lemon.

“Some boats are speedy. Some boats are strong. This boat is a lemon.”

Arlo giggled. Ruby and Riley sniffed the lemon and decided it was not a ball.

The Best Yard Boat

Arlo looked at the boat beneath the tree.

“Maybe it doesn’t want to be a water boat,” he said. “Maybe it likes being a yard boat.”

Grumpy considered this.

The boat was terrible at starting. Terrible at floating anywhere useful. Terrible at bringing Grumpy home.

But it was excellent at sitting quietly in the shade.

Grumpy leaned back. Gaga cuddled Arlo. Ruby and Riley curled up in the grass.

Zzzzzzz…

And the yard boat showed everyone exactly what it did best.
