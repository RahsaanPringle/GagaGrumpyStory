Something in the Garden

Gaga grew lettuce, tomatoes, beans, and bright yellow flowers in her garden.

One morning, she heard a suspicious sound among the vegetables.

Scratch-scratch! Cluck-cluck!

There were Gaga’s chickens—stepping on lettuce, bumping the beans, and kicking dirt over everything.

Riley stared. Little Ruby stared.

Gaga stared hardest of all.

¡Insectos!

Riley stepped to the garden gate.

“Goldie, what are you doing in Gaga’s vegetables?” she asked.

Goldie scratched beneath a leaf and found a wiggly worm.

“¡Insectos!”

The other chickens clucked together.

“¡Sí! ¡Insectos!”

Ruby blinked. “What did they say?”

Riley Translates

“They are looking for insects to eat,” Riley explained.

Insectos means insects.
Sí means yes.

Ruby looked at the bent lettuce. “But the insects are hiding in Gaga’s garden.”

Riley nodded.

Jardín means garden.

“The chickens may like the jardín,” Riley said, “but Gaga’s vegetables do not like chicken feet.”

Everybody Out!

Riley walked slowly behind the chickens.

Ruby hurried to the other side of the gate.

“This way, Goldie,” said Riley.

“Not that way,” said Ruby.

The hens waddled, flapped, and clucked toward the opening.

Paso, paso, paso!

Paso means step.

Soon, ten chicken feet had stepped safely out of the garden.

Ruby’s Better Place

Ruby sniffed beside an old log in the grass.

She lifted a big leaf with her nose.

Underneath were a beetle, a cricket, and one fat worm.

Goldie’s eyes grew wide.

“You can look for insects here,” Ruby told the flock. “The yard has plenty!”

¡Insectos en el patio!

Patio means yard.
Insectos en el patio means insects in the yard.

Everyone’s Happy Place

The chickens scratched through the soft grass.

They found beetles beneath leaves and worms near the old log.

Gaga’s vegetables stood safely behind the garden gate.

Riley rested beside Ruby while Gaga showed Arlo the garden.

Goldie clucked, “¡Gracias, amigas!”

Gracias means thank you.
Amigas means female friends.

The chickens had their insectos. Gaga had her jardín. And Ruby and Riley had done a very good job.
