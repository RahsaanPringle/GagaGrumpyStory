The Sound of Home

“You were snoring,” Arlo told him.

“I do not snore,” said Grumpy. “I have never snored in my whole life.”

He closed his eyes again. Arlo curled up beside him. Ruby and Riley stretched out near the chair.

Soon, Grumpy’s familiar rumble filled the room.

HOOONK… SHOOO!

Arlo smiled. It was the sound of being safe at Grumpy and Gaga’s house.

And Grumpy kept snoring.
