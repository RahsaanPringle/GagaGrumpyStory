Grumpy sat back in his soft, cozy chair.

Gaga and Milo were both sitting there.

“I'm just resting my eyes,” Grumpy said with a grin.

Then, three seconds later, the snores tumbled in.


Hooonk... shooo! What a rumbling roar!

Milo had never heard that sound before.

“A bear?” whispered Gaga. “Could one be in here?”

But Grumpy kept snoring, with nothing to fear.


Milo peeked round the curtains to see what was there.

No paws and no claws and no big furry bear.

He listened again. There was something brand-new:

A rumble, a putt-putt, a puff, and a shooo!


“A tractor!” said Gaga. He looked out to see.

A red one stood out by a green leafy tree.

But this rumble came from the room, loud and clear.

Then chugga-chugga... whoooo! Was a train coming near?

No train by the window. No tracks on the floor.

Just Grumpy, still sleeping, who snored a bit more.


Now Ruby and Riley came close, nose to nose.

They sniffed at his whiskers. They sniffed at his clothes.

Hooonk... shooo! Both dogs perked up an ear.

The bear and the train and the tractor were here!

No need to keep looking. No need to explore.

Those funny old sounds were all Grumpy's big snore.


Then snork! Grumpy woke with his hand in the air.

Two dogs, one small Milo, and Gaga were there.

“What's all this noise? Can't a fellow just rest?”

Milo looked up at the Grumpy he loved best.

“You snored!” Milo told him. “We heard every peep!”

“Who, me?” Grumpy said. “I was hardly asleep!”


Then Milo curled up, with his cheek on his chest.

And Ruby and Riley lay down for a rest.

The house settled down for a soft, snuggly sleep.

The dogs at his feet never stirred in their heap.

Hooonk... shooo! Such a soft, sleepy song.

Safe beside Grumpy was where they belonged.

Gaga stayed close as they slumbered some more.

And Grumpy kept snoring his soft, rumbly snore.
