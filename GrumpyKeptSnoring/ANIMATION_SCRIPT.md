# Grumpy Keeps Snoring — Animation Script

## Production setup

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps
- **Estimated runtime:** 80–95 seconds
- **Visual direction:** Follow `STYLE_GUIDE.md`; use canonical characters and keep Milo about 3 years old.
- **Animation approach:** Cozy watercolor-and-ink animation with close reaction shots, restrained facial motion, and playful imagined sounds. The bear, tractor, and train remain off-screen ideas, not literal intrusions into the room.
- **Audio:** Warm narrator, comic snore performance, soft indoor ambience, and playful sound-design transformations.
- **Transitions:** Sound bridges connect scenes; camera movement stays gentle.

## Scene 1 — Just resting his eyes

- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Narration:** “After lunch Grumpy settled into his favorite chair. ‘I’m not taking a nap. I’m only resting my eyes.’ For three whole seconds, the room was quiet.”
- **Picture:** Grumpy reclines while Gaga and Milo listen nearby.
- **Motion:** Grumpy raises one finger, closes his eyes, and relaxes; Milo watches closely.
- **Camera:** Medium shot with a slow push during the three-second silence.
- **Sound:** Clock tick, room tone, then an intake of breath.
- **Transition:** First snore begins before the cut.

## Scene 2 — What is that noise?

- **Duration:** 11 seconds
- **Narration:** “HOOONK… SHOOO! Milo's eyes grew wide. ‘What was that?’ Gaga listened. ‘It sounded like a bear.’ But Grumpy kept snoring.”
- **Picture:** Grumpy sleeps as Gaga and Milo exchange surprised looks.
- **Motion:** Grumpy's chest rises; mustache and beard flutter slightly; Milo's eyes widen; Gaga cups a hand to her ear.
- **Camera:** Close-up sequence ending on Grumpy, completely peaceful.
- **Sound:** Large comic snore with a soft bear-like rumble underneath.
- **Transition:** Milo follows the imagined sound toward the curtains.

## Scene 3 — Looking for a bear

- **Duration:** 11 seconds
- **Narration:** “Milo looked behind the curtains, beneath the table, and inside the toy basket. No paws, no claws, no furry bear. Then the noise changed.”
- **Picture:** Milo searches the room while Grumpy sleeps in the background.
- **Motion:** Curtain lifts, Milo crawls to check under the table, and toys shift in the basket.
- **Camera:** Low toddler-height tracking shot through the search.
- **Sound:** Soft tiptoe notes leading into “rumble-rumble, putt-putt-putt.”
- **Transition:** Milo turns toward the window.

## Scene 4 — A tractor? A train?

- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Narration:** “‘Now it sounds like a tractor,’ Gaga said. Milo saw birds, trees, and sunshine—but no tractor. Then came a train sound, although no tracks ran near Grumpy's chair.”
- **Picture:** Milo peers through the window at the peaceful yard.
- **Motion:** A distant tractor moves quietly far away only if already part of the view; Milo scans left and right as curtains sway.
- **Camera:** Over Milo's shoulder to the window, then pan back to Grumpy.
- **Sound:** Snore shifts from tractor rhythm to “chugga-chugga… whooooo!”
- **Transition:** Dogs' ears rise at the train-like whistle.

## Scene 5 — Ruby and Riley investigate

- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Narration:** “Ruby and Riley came running. They sniffed Grumpy’s slippers and inspected his ears. Riley cautiously sniffed his open mouth. The bear, tractor, and train were all coming from Grumpy!”
- **Picture:** Both dogs investigate the sleeping Grumpy.
- **Motion:** Ruby circles the slippers; Riley leans toward Grumpy's mouth; both recoil slightly at the next snore and look at Milo.
- **Camera:** Series of dog-height close-ups followed by a wide reaction shot.
- **Sound:** Sniffs, collar tags, one enormous “HOOONK… SHOOO!”
- **Transition:** Everyone leans toward Grumpy in a synchronized move.

## Scene 6 — Grumpy wakes up

- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Narration:** “Milo leaned closer. Ruby, Riley, and Gaga leaned closer too. SNORK! Grumpy's eyes flew open. ‘Why is everybody making so much noise? I’m trying to rest!’”
- **Picture:** The family crowds around the chair just before Grumpy wakes.
- **Motion:** Slow lean-in, sudden eye-open and snort, Milo hops back, dogs scramble, Gaga sits down laughing.
- **Camera:** Slow zoom in followed by a quick safe pullback on “SNORK!”
- **Sound:** Snort, soft scramble, Gaga's laughter.
- **Transition:** Laughter dissolves into the quiet final scene.

## Scene 7 — The sound of home

- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Narration:** “‘You were snoring,’ Milo said. ‘I do not snore,’ Grumpy replied. Soon Milo curled beside him, the dogs stretched out nearby, and Grumpy's familiar rumble filled the room.”
- **Picture:** Milo sleeps safely against Grumpy while Gaga and the dogs stay close.
- **Motion:** Milo settles, dogs lie down, Gaga smiles, and everyone's breathing becomes slow and even.
- **Camera:** Close on Milo's smile, then slow pullback across the whole room.
- **Sound:** Gentle “HOOONK… SHOOO!” becomes part of the closing lullaby.
- **Transition:** Fade to warm amber.
