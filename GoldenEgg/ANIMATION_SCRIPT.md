# Ruby, Riley & Goldie's Egg — Animation Script

## Production setup

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps
- **Estimated runtime:** 85–100 seconds
- **Visual direction:** Follow `STYLE_GUIDE.md`; use canonical Goldie, Riley, Ruby, Gaga, and one-year-old Milo.
- **Animation approach:** Bright watercolor-and-ink animation with readable animal acting, lively but safe chase motion, and restrained background parallax.
- **Audio:** Warm narrator, playful acoustic score, chicken clucks, paw beats, and clear Spanish pronunciation.
- **Transitions:** Quick action wipes during the chase; soft dissolves during the lesson and ending.

## Scene 1 — Gaga's chickens

- **Duration:** 13 seconds
- **Narration:** “Every morning Gaga carried feed to her chickens. Riley, two years old and fully grown, sat quietly. Ruby was nine months old—smaller than Riley, but full of wiggles.”
- **Picture:** Gaga scatters feed while Riley sits and Ruby watches the flock intensely.
- **Motion:** Chickens peck; Riley's tail rests; Ruby's ears rise and tail speeds up; Gaga notices the change.
- **Camera:** Wide yard view followed by a push toward Ruby's excited face.
- **Sound:** Feed rattle, soft clucking, tail swishes.
- **Transition:** Ruby crouches and springs into the next scene.

## Scene 2 — Ruby runs

- **Duration:** 11 seconds
- **Narration:** “One hen hopped left. Another flapped right. ‘Ruby, wait!’ called Gaga. But Ruby was already running. Zoom! Flap! Cluck-cluck-cluck!”
- **Picture:** Ruby joyfully chases the startled chickens across the grass.
- **Motion:** Chickens scatter safely; Ruby bounds after them; Gaga reaches out; Riley rises.
- **Camera:** Fast side tracking with a short whip pan to Riley.
- **Sound:** Wing flaps, clucks, paw beats, comic percussion.
- **Transition:** Riley crosses frame at speed.

## Scene 3 — Riley runs too

- **Duration:** 12 seconds
- **Narration:** “The chickens ran from Ruby. Ruby ran after the chickens. And Riley ran after Ruby. Riley was bigger, older, faster—and knew where Ruby was going before Ruby did.”
- **Picture:** The chase circles the coop, water bucket, and lawn.
- **Motion:** Ruby takes a tight curve; Riley chooses a shorter path and moves ahead.
- **Camera:** Slight overhead view that makes the chase path easy to follow.
- **Sound:** Rhythmic paw beats, clucks, rising playful music.
- **Transition:** Riley slides into position between Ruby and the flock.

## Scene 4 — Chickens are friends

- **Duration:** 16 seconds
- **Narration:** “‘Why did you stop me?’ Ruby asked. ‘They were playing chase!’ Riley explained, ‘They were frightened. Gaga’s chickens are family. We watch over our friends. We do not chase them.’”
- **Picture:** Riley stands calmly between Ruby and the chickens.
- **Motion:** Ruby skids to a stop, looks from Riley to the nervous flock, and lowers her ears apologetically.
- **Camera:** Medium three-character triangle; gentle push toward Ruby's realization.
- **Sound:** Music settles; one quiet cluck.
- **Transition:** Goldie steps forward into a soft spotlight of daylight.

## Scene 5 — Goldie's gift

- **Duration:** 16 seconds
- **Narration:** “Goldie stepped from the flock and placed a brown egg in a nest. ‘¡Amiga!’ she said. Amiga means a female friend in Spanish.”
- **Picture:** Goldie presents the egg while Ruby and Riley watch respectfully.
- **Motion:** Goldie nudges straw around the egg, looks at Ruby, and gives one proud cluck; Ruby tilts her head.
- **Camera:** Close on egg and Goldie, then widen to include the dogs.
- **Sound:** Gentle cluck, soft musical chime; pronounce “amiga” clearly.
- **Transition:** Dissolve from the egg's warm brown to afternoon sunlight.

## Scene 6 — Amigas

- **Duration:** 18 seconds
- **Narration:** “Ruby gave Goldie the gentlest sniff. ‘Amiga,’ she repeated. From then on, Ruby rested beside the chickens instead of chasing them. She had gained five friends—and one new Spanish word.”
- **Picture:** Ruby, Riley, Goldie, the flock, Gaga, and baby Milo relax together.
- **Motion:** Ruby sniffs gently; Goldie clucks; Riley watches with one eye open; Gaga shows the egg to Milo.
- **Camera:** Start close on Ruby and Goldie, then pull back to the peaceful group.
- **Sound:** Contented clucks, breeze, warm musical resolution.
- **Transition:** Fade to sunny cream.
