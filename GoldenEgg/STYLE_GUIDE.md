# Ruby, Riley & Goldie's Egg — Visual Style Guide

## Current artwork

- **Style family:** comic digital gouache
- **Medium:** opaque digital paint with light watercolor grain
- **Linework:** clear medium-weight dark outlines around characters and important props
- **Color:** sunny greens, sky blue, barn red, golden yellow, and warm tan
- **Lighting:** bright even daylight with minimal shadow contrast
- **Characters:** friendly cartoon anatomy, large eyes, expressive ears and tails, readable silhouettes
- **Arlo's approximate age:** about 1 year old; a baby or young toddler who sits securely with Gaga and handles the egg with help
- **Backgrounds:** detailed but orderly fenced yard, coop, flowers, and grass
- **Composition:** six 16:9 panels in a 2-by-3 sprite sheet; action flows clearly from left to right
- **Mood:** lively, funny, kind, and educational

## Direction for new artwork

Keep the bright comic readability while moving fully into the shared watercolor-and-ink style. Use `../Characters/Goldie.png`, `Riley.png`, `Ruby.png`, `Gaga.png`, and `Arlo.png` as identity references. Draw Arlo at about age 1 with a large head, short limbs, round cheeks, and supported sitting or carried poses. Use the chicken area shown in `../Scenes/Terrain-Storybook.png` for consistent geography.

Goldie is the golden-buff hen with a red comb. Keep Riley larger with a green collar and Ruby smaller with a red collar. For sprite sheets, use equal 16:9 panels in a 2-by-3 grid.

## Avoid

Photorealistic chickens, generic dog breeds, swapped collar colors, cluttered flocks that hide the action, heavy shadows, glossy 3D rendering, and speech text unless the story explicitly requires it.
