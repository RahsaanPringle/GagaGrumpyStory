# Ruby, Riley & Goldie’s Egg — YouTube Description

Ruby is full of puppy wiggles when she spots Gaga’s chickens scratching and fluttering across the yard. She thinks they are playing chase—but Riley understands that the frightened chickens need a friend to protect them.

Then Goldie, the golden hen, steps forward with a special gift and one important Spanish word: **¡Amiga!** With Riley’s help, Ruby learns to listen, be gentle, and treat every member of the family with care.

**Ruby, Riley & Goldie’s Egg** is a warm illustrated story about friendship, empathy, and learning that an *amiga* is a female friend in Spanish.

Made with love for Milo, Ruby, Riley, Gaga, Goldie—and all their amigas.
