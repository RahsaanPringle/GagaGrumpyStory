Gaga’s Chickens

Every morning, Gaga carried a bucket of feed to her chickens.

Riley, who was two years old and fully grown, sat quietly beside her.

Ruby was only nine months old. She was smaller than Riley, but she had more wiggles than three dogs put together.

When Ruby saw the chickens scratching and fluttering, her ears stood tall. Her tail began to swish.

Riley knew that look.

Ruby Runs!

One golden hen hopped to the left.

Another chicken flapped to the right.

Ruby bounced forward.

“Ruby, wait!” called Gaga.

But Ruby was already running.

Zoom! Flap! Cluck-cluck-cluck!

The chickens hurried across the yard, and Ruby hurried right behind them.

Riley Runs Too

Riley sprang to her feet.

The chickens ran from Ruby.

Ruby ran after the chickens.

And Riley ran after Ruby.

Around the coop they went. Across the grass they flew. Past the water bucket they raced.

Riley was bigger. Riley was older. Riley was faster.

Most importantly, Riley knew where Ruby was going before Ruby did.

Chickens Are Friends

Riley hurried around Ruby and stood between her and the chickens.

Ruby skidded to a stop.

“Why did you stop me?” Ruby asked. “They were playing chase!”

Riley shook her head.

“They weren’t playing,” she explained. “They were frightened. Gaga’s chickens are part of our family. We watch over our friends. We do not chase them.”

Ruby looked at the chickens. She had not meant to frighten anyone.

Goldie’s Gift

Then the golden hen stepped out from the flock.

Her name was Goldie.

Goldie placed a beautiful brown egg in a little nest of straw. She looked at Ruby and said:

“¡Amiga!”

Ruby blinked. “What does that mean?”

Amiga means female friend in Spanish. Goldie is a girl, and Ruby is a girl, so Goldie called Ruby her amiga. A boy friend would be an amigo.

Amigas

Ruby lowered her nose and gave Goldie the gentlest sniff she had ever given anyone.

“Amiga,” Ruby repeated.

Goldie clucked happily.

From that day on, Ruby did not chase Gaga’s chickens. She rested beside them while they scratched in the grass. Riley still kept one watchful eye open—just in case Ruby’s puppy wiggles returned.

Gaga showed the egg to Arlo, and everyone enjoyed the peaceful afternoon.

Ruby had lost a game of chase, but she had gained five new friends—and one new Spanish word.
