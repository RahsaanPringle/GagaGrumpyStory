# Ruby, Riley & the Scent of Home — Animation Script

## Production setup

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps
- **Estimated runtime:** 85–100 seconds
- **Visual direction:** Follow `STYLE_GUIDE.md`; use canonical characters and keep Milo about 4 years old.
- **Animation approach:** Warm watercolor-and-ink animation with dog-height tracking shots, subtle yard parallax, and a restrained golden dotted scent trail.
- **Audio:** Warm narrator, playful sniffing sounds, light acoustic music, and clear Spanish pronunciation.
- **Transitions:** Scent-trail wipes and soft dissolves; sunset color gradually warms across the story.

## Scene 1 — Ready, set, hide

- **Duration:** 14 seconds
- **Narration:** “Ruby and Riley loved nose-work. Grumpy held them on the porch while Gaga and Milo carried little cups of cheese to their hiding places. ‘Ready… set… sniff!’”
- **Picture:** Grumpy waits with the dogs as Gaga and Milo head into the yard.
- **Motion:** Noses twitch, ears rise, Milo carries his cup carefully, and Grumpy releases the collars on “sniff.”
- **Camera:** Wide porch shot, then close inserts of two noses and four ears.
- **Sound:** Cup rattle, collar tags, three-beat musical countdown.
- **Transition:** Golden scent dots drift into the next shot.

## Scene 2 — Follow your noses

- **Duration:** 13 seconds
- **Narration:** “Riley followed one careful step at a time. Ruby zigged toward a flower, zagged toward the shed, then hurried back. Was the smell Milo? Was it cheese? It was both!”
- **Picture:** The dogs track across the yard while Milo hides beside the tree.
- **Motion:** Riley follows a steady line; Ruby takes comic detours; both trails converge at the tree.
- **Camera:** Low tracking shot with a brief overhead view of the scent paths.
- **Sound:** Sniff-sniff, snuffle-snuffle, grass rustle.
- **Transition:** Ruby's nose rounds the tree trunk into Milo's reveal.

## Scene 3 — They found Milo

- **Duration:** 13 seconds
- **Narration:** “‘You found me!’ Milo laughed. He gave each dog a tiny piece of cheese. Queso means cheese. ‘Good sniffing! Now go find Gaga!’”
- **Picture:** Milo rewards the dogs beside the tree.
- **Motion:** Ruby wiggles nose-to-tail; Riley wags; Milo offers one small treat to each and points toward the flowers.
- **Camera:** Medium shot at Milo's height; short close-up on the treats.
- **Sound:** Happy panting, tiny treat crunches; pronounce “queso” clearly.
- **Transition:** Dogs run past camera; wipe with their moving tails.

## Scene 4 — What if they forget me?

- **Duration:** 16 seconds
- **Narration:** “As the dogs followed Gaga’s scent, Milo's smile slipped away. ‘What if they forget about me?’ Grumpy knelt beside him. ‘Looking for Gaga does not mean they stopped loving you. A heart has room for everyone.’”
- **Picture:** Milo watches the dogs disappear behind flowers while Grumpy reassures him.
- **Motion:** Milo's shoulders lower; Grumpy kneels and offers his hand; Milo takes it.
- **Camera:** Slow push from the distant dogs to Milo and Grumpy in the foreground.
- **Sound:** Music softens; breeze through flowers.
- **Transition:** A flower crosses frame and reveals Gaga.

## Scene 5 — They found Gaga

- **Duration:** 14 seconds
- **Narration:** “Behind the tallest flowers, there was Gaga! She cheered their wonderful noses and gave each dog a treat. Then both dogs looked back toward Milo. They remembered exactly where he was.”
- **Picture:** Riley and Ruby find Gaga beside the flowers; Milo and Grumpy watch nearby.
- **Motion:** Tails thump, Ruby bounces, Gaga rewards them, and both dogs turn their heads toward Milo together.
- **Camera:** Reveal Gaga from behind flowers; pan along the dogs' eyeline back to Milo.
- **Sound:** Happy musical lift, tail thumps, light laugh.
- **Transition:** Sunset warmth fills the frame.

## Scene 6 — ¡A casa!

- **Duration:** 18 seconds
- **Narration:** “From the porch Grumpy called, ‘Everybody—¡a casa!’ Home! Gaga came from the flowers. Ruby and Riley raced beside her. Milo ran to meet them. The people who love you always come back together.”
- **Picture:** At sunset the family returns through the open door.
- **Motion:** Grumpy waves from the porch; Milo runs forward; Gaga and the dogs cross the yard; everyone gathers at the doorway.
- **Camera:** Wide sunset view that slowly moves toward the warmly lit door.
- **Sound:** Evening birds, running paws, closing musical theme; pronounce “a casa” clearly.
- **Transition:** Fade through the warm doorway light.
