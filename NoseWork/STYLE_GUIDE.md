# Ruby, Riley & the Scent of Home — Visual Style Guide

## Current artwork

- **Style family:** warm comic storybook painting
- **Medium:** digital gouache with light paper grain and softly blended shading
- **Linework:** thin-to-medium dark outlines, with simplified lines in distant foliage
- **Color:** olive and lawn green, barn red, sky blue, burgundy, and sunset gold
- **Lighting:** clear afternoon daylight that transitions to warm sunset
- **Characters:** approachable cartoon realism with expressive eyes, ears, hands, and posture
- **Arlo's approximate age:** about 4 years old; a mobile preschooler who can follow directions, carry treats, and express a complex worry
- **Backgrounds:** consistent backyard, porch, mature tree, fence, flower beds, and coop
- **Composition:** six 16:9 panels in a 2-by-3 sprite sheet; scent trails may use simple glowing dotted paths
- **Mood:** curious, reassuring, connected, and warmly adventurous

## Direction for new artwork

Use canonical character files from `../Characters/` and the shared watercolor-and-ink finish. Draw Arlo at about age 4 with a preschooler's large head, shorter legs, energetic gait, and open facial expressions. Retain the story's clear spatial staging and warm sunset ending. Outdoor layouts should follow `../Scenes/Terrain-Storybook.png` loosely enough to support the scene while keeping recognizable landmarks.

Keep Riley larger with a green collar and Ruby smaller with a red collar. Use scent lines sparingly as a soft golden dotted trail. For sprite sheets, use equal 16:9 panels in a 2-by-3 grid.

## Avoid

Opaque magical smoke, neon effects, confusing scent paths, photorealistic characters, swapped collar colors, overly dense backgrounds, glossy 3D rendering, and changing the house or coop between panels.
