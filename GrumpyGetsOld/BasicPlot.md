# Grumpy Gets Old

A rhyme about watching the people you know and love age in real time.

When you look at Grumpy, what do you see?
The face in place will change, you see.
 As time goes on, the Grump will change.
 And as you watch, you'll rearrange.


 I feel like this could roll along with the song "Old man look at my life" in some way. It's a person looking into a future, as if it had not seen the past.
