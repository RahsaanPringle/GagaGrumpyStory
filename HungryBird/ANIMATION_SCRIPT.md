# Ruby, Riley & the Hungry Chickens — Animation Script

## Production setup

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps
- **Estimated runtime:** 85–105 seconds
- **Visual direction:** Follow `STYLE_GUIDE.md`; use canonical characters and keep Milo about 1 year old.
- **Animation approach:** Colorful watercolor-and-ink motion with clear garden geography, gentle flock movement, and readable educational beats.
- **Audio:** Warm narrator, light acoustic music, garden ambience, chicken sounds, and careful Spanish pronunciation.
- **Transitions:** Leaf wipes, garden-object match cuts, and soft dissolves.

## Scene 1 — Something in the garden

- **Duration:** 14 seconds
- **Narration:** “Gaga grew lettuce, tomatoes, beans, and yellow flowers. One morning she heard scratch-scratch, cluck-cluck. Her chickens were stepping on lettuce, bumping beans, and kicking dirt everywhere.”
- **Picture:** Gaga discovers the flock inside the vegetable beds while Riley and Ruby stare.
- **Motion:** Chickens scratch and peck; a bean vine wobbles; Gaga and both dogs lean forward in synchronized surprise.
- **Camera:** Begin on the peaceful garden, then pan to reveal the chickens.
- **Sound:** Garden birds, scratching, layered clucks, short comic sting.
- **Transition:** Push through the open garden gate.

## Scene 2 — ¡Insectos!

- **Duration:** 14 seconds
- **Narration:** “‘Goldie, what are you doing?’ Riley asked. Goldie found a wiggly worm. ‘¡Insectos!’ The flock agreed. ‘¡Sí! ¡Insectos!’”
- **Picture:** Goldie scratches beneath a leaf and proudly reveals a worm.
- **Motion:** Riley approaches calmly; Goldie lifts the leaf; chickens nod and cluck; Ruby blinks in confusion.
- **Camera:** Low close-up on the leaf, then rack attention to Ruby.
- **Sound:** Scratch, worm wiggle accent, cheerful clucks; pronounce “insectos” and “sí” clearly.
- **Transition:** Ruby's questioning look cuts to Riley explaining.

## Scene 3 — Riley translates

- **Duration:** 16 seconds
- **Narration:** “‘They are looking for insects,’ Riley explained. Insectos means insects. Sí means yes. The chickens liked the jardín—but Gaga’s vegetables did not like chicken feet.”
- **Picture:** Riley explains while Gaga gently straightens a bent plant.
- **Motion:** Riley gestures with nose and paw; Ruby studies the footprints; Gaga props up the lettuce.
- **Camera:** Medium group shot with inserts of footprints and vegetables.
- **Sound:** Soft educational music; garden ambience continues.
- **Transition:** Gate swings open toward the camera.

## Scene 4 — Everybody out

- **Duration:** 14 seconds
- **Narration:** “Riley walked behind the chickens. Ruby hurried to the other side. Paso, paso, paso! Soon ten chicken feet had stepped safely out of the garden.”
- **Picture:** The dogs gently guide the flock through the gate.
- **Motion:** Riley advances slowly; Ruby blocks the wrong path; five hens waddle out in a tidy, comic line.
- **Camera:** Overhead angle makes the herding pattern clear, then lower to chicken height at the gate.
- **Sound:** Foot taps, wing rustles, “paso, paso, paso” in rhythm.
- **Transition:** Follow Ruby's nose down toward an old log.

## Scene 5 — Ruby's better place

- **Duration:** 16 seconds
- **Narration:** “Ruby lifted a leaf beside an old log. Underneath were a beetle, a cricket, and one fat worm. ‘You can look here,’ she told the flock. ‘¡Insectos en el patio!’”
- **Picture:** Ruby reveals the insects in a safe patch of yard as Goldie's eyes widen.
- **Motion:** Leaf lifts, insects scuttle gently, Goldie leans closer, and the flock hurries over without chasing.
- **Camera:** Macro-style close shot under the leaf, then widen to Ruby and Goldie.
- **Sound:** Cricket chirp, delighted clucks; pronounce the Spanish phrase clearly.
- **Transition:** Dissolve from the log to the peaceful wide yard.

## Scene 6 — Everyone's happy place

- **Duration:** 18 seconds
- **Narration:** “The chickens found insects in the yard. Gaga’s vegetables stayed safe behind the gate. ‘¡Gracias, amigas!’ Goldie clucked. The chickens had their insectos, Gaga had her jardín, and Ruby and Riley had done a very good job.”
- **Picture:** Chickens forage by the log; Gaga shows the garden to baby Milo; the dogs rest nearby.
- **Motion:** Calm scratching, Riley and Ruby settle side by side, Gaga points out a tomato to Milo.
- **Camera:** Slow lateral move connecting yard, gate, garden, and family.
- **Sound:** Gentle clucks, breeze, warm closing music.
- **Transition:** Fade to pale garden green.
