# Ruby, Riley & the Hungry Chickens — Visual Style Guide

## Current artwork

- **Style family:** comic digital gouache
- **Medium:** opaque digital paint with a subtle watercolor-paper texture
- **Linework:** medium dark outlines, strongest on characters and foreground garden objects
- **Color:** leafy greens, tomato red, warm soil brown, golden feathers, and clear blue sky
- **Lighting:** bright cheerful daylight with soft ground shadows
- **Characters:** friendly cartoon proportions and strongly readable animal expressions
- **Milo's approximate age:** about 1 year old; a baby or young toddler whose participation is mostly observational and supported by Gaga
- **Backgrounds:** richly planted vegetable beds, wooden fencing, coop, lawn, and flowers
- **Composition:** six 16:9 panels in a 2-by-3 sprite sheet; layered foreground garden detail supports each action
- **Mood:** busy, playful, helpful, and celebratory

## Direction for new artwork

Use the shared watercolor-and-ink series style and canonical assets from `../Characters/`. When Milo appears, draw him at about age 1 with baby proportions, supported sitting or carried poses, and simple delighted reactions. Keep garden scenes colorful and detailed enough to show vegetables and bugs, while maintaining a single obvious story action. Match the chicken-yard placement to `../Scenes/Terrain-Storybook.png`.

Keep Riley larger with a green collar and Ruby smaller with a red collar. For sprite sheets, use equal 16:9 panels in a 2-by-3 grid.

## Avoid

Overcrowded compositions, realistic predatory behavior, frightening animal expressions, muddy greens, swapped dog proportions or collars, photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, and unreadably tiny insects when they matter to the scene.
