# Shared story setting

Use `Terrain-Storybook.png` as the visual geography reference for new story scenes. The layout can bend to fit a composition, but the main relationships should remain recognizable:

- Grumpy's house sits beside the wooded edge of GrumpyLand.
- GrumpyLand contains the woods, grassy clearings, stream, trails, and footbridge.
- Gaga's house sits across the curving country road, surrounded by flowers and a garden.
- The chicken coop and fenced chicken yard sit beyond Gaga's house toward the upper-right side of the property.
- Paths and driveways connect the houses, chicken area, road, and GrumpyLand.

`Terrain.png` is the original annotated aerial reference. `00-cover.png` and `01-big-news.png` are group-scene style references. Individual reusable character cutouts live in `../Characters/`.
