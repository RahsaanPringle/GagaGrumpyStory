# Gaga Went Away — Animation Script

## Production setup

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps
- **Estimated runtime:** 85–105 seconds
- **Visual direction:** Follow `STYLE_GUIDE.md`; Milo is about 5 years old. Keep the imagery literal, calm, and emotionally safe.
- **Animation approach:** Restrained watercolor-and-ink motion, quiet gestures, slow camera moves, and gentle environmental movement. Do not visualize death, illness, heaven, or the “rainbow bridge.”
- **Audio:** Calm narrator, sparse piano and warm strings, soft home and garden ambience. Leave room for silence.
- **Transitions:** Slow dissolves and gentle light changes; no abrupt cuts except Milo calling into the empty house.

## Scene 1 — Where is Gaga?

- **Duration:** 13 seconds
- **Narration:** “Milo hurried up the path to visit Gaga and Grumpy. Grumpy opened the door. Riley wagged. Ruby wiggled. Milo hugged everyone—then looked past them. ‘Where is Gaga?’”
- **Picture:** Milo arrives with his backpack and greets Grumpy and the dogs at the doorway.
- **Motion:** Backpack bounces, dogs greet him, Grumpy kneels for a hug, then everyone becomes still when Milo looks inside.
- **Camera:** Follow Milo up the path; settle into an eye-level doorway composition.
- **Sound:** Footsteps, collar tags, greeting music that gently thins.
- **Transition:** Dissolve from the open doorway to Gaga's empty chair.

## Scene 2 — Milo asks Riley

- **Duration:** 13 seconds
- **Narration:** “Milo checked beside Gaga’s favorite chair and near the window. ‘Riley, where is Gaga?’ Riley rested her head beside him. ‘Gaga went away,’ she said softly.”
- **Picture:** Milo and Riley sit beside the empty chair in warm window light.
- **Motion:** Curtain moves slightly; Milo looks around; Riley lowers her head onto his knee.
- **Camera:** Slow pan from empty chair to Milo and Riley.
- **Sound:** Quiet room tone and a few piano notes.
- **Transition:** Flower colors from the window dissolve into the garden.

## Scene 3 — Milo asks Ruby

- **Duration:** 13 seconds
- **Narration:** “Milo searched among Gaga’s flowers. ‘Ruby, where did Gaga go?’ Ruby sat very still. ‘She’s over the rainbow bridge,’ she said. ‘But where?’ Milo asked.”
- **Picture:** Milo kneels among the flowers with Ruby beside him.
- **Motion:** Leaves move in a soft breeze; Ruby's ears lower; Milo gently parts the flowers and looks beyond them.
- **Camera:** Low garden-level move ending on Milo's questioning face.
- **Sound:** Bees and birds, with the score kept quiet.
- **Transition:** Slow dissolve into the kitchen hallway.

## Scene 4 — Milo keeps looking

- **Duration:** 13 seconds
- **Narration:** “Milo looked in the kitchen and down the hallway. ‘Gaga! I’m here!’ But Gaga did not answer. Riley stayed on one side. Ruby stayed on the other.”
- **Picture:** Milo searches familiar rooms while the dogs remain close.
- **Motion:** He checks a doorway, calls, waits, and slowly lowers his shoulders; each dog presses gently against one side.
- **Camera:** Two slow tracking shots joined by one direct cut after his call.
- **Sound:** His voice echoes lightly; then silence and soft collar movement.
- **Transition:** Grumpy's hand enters frame and leads into the next scene.

## Scene 5 — Grumpy tells the truth

- **Duration:** 20 seconds
- **Narration:** “Grumpy held Milo close. ‘Gaga was very sick with cancer. The doctors tried to help, but her body stopped working. Gaga died. Nothing you said, thought, or did caused it.’”
- **Picture:** Grumpy and Milo sit together; the dogs rest nearby; Gaga's photograph is visible but not emphasized.
- **Motion:** Grumpy opens his arms, Milo leans in, and Grumpy speaks with calm eye contact. Milo's breathing settles.
- **Camera:** Locked medium shot with a very slow push; do not cut during the explanation.
- **Sound:** Narration remains clear over nearly silent music.
- **Transition:** Photograph catches warm light and dissolves into the family album.

## Scene 6 — Gaga is still in our story

- **Duration:** 20 seconds
- **Narration:** “‘Gaga’s love did not disappear,’ Grumpy said. ‘She is with you in the stories she told, the things she taught you, and every loving memory you carry.’ Milo would never have to remember her alone.”
- **Picture:** At sunset, Milo and Grumpy look through the album while Riley and Ruby lean close.
- **Motion:** A page turns to pictures of flowers, chickens, hugs, and laughter; Milo and Grumpy wipe tears and hold each other; Ruby places a paw on Milo's knee.
- **Camera:** Close on the album, then slow pullback to the full family group.
- **Sound:** Gentle page turn, warm strings, evening birds.
- **Transition:** Fade into sunset gold; no symbolic rainbow imagery.
