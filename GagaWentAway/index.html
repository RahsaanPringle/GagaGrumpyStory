Arlo Keeps Looking

Arlo looked in the kitchen.

He looked down the hallway.

He even called, “Gaga! I’m here!”

But Gaga did not answer.

Riley stayed on one side of him. Ruby stayed on the other.

Arlo’s eyes filled with tears.

“Grumpy,” he said, “please tell me where Gaga went.”

Grumpy Tells the Truth

Grumpy sat beside Arlo and held him close.

“Gaga was very sick with cancer,” he said. “The doctors tried to help, but her body stopped working. Gaga died.”

When someone dies, their body does not breathe, eat, talk, or feel pain anymore. They cannot come back the way they used to.

“Did I make it happen?” Arlo asked.

“No,” said Grumpy. “Nothing you said, thought, or did caused it. There is nothing that you could have done to stop it.”

Gaga Is Still in Our Story

“You cannot see Gaga or talk with her the way you used to,” Grumpy said. “But her love did not disappear.”

“Gaga is with you in the stories she told, the things she taught you, and every loving memory you carry.”

They opened the family album. They remembered Gaga’s flowers, Gaga’s chickens, Gaga’s hugs, and Gaga’s laugh.

Arlo cried because he missed her. Grumpy cried too.

Riley leaned close. Ruby put one big puppy paw on Arlo’s knee.

Gaga could not be there the old way. But Arlo would never have to remember her alone.
